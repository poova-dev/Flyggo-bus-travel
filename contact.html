❓ FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I cancel my Flyggo ticket? ▼
Go to "My Bookings" in your account, select the booking and click "Cancel Ticket". Refund is processed within 24–48 hours to your original payment method. Free cancellation is available up to 2 hours before departure.
Where do I collect my e-ticket? ▼
Your e-ticket is sent instantly to your registered email and mobile number via SMS. You can also find it under "My Bookings" in your Flyggo account. No printout is required — show the e-ticket on your mobile at the boarding point.
What is the luggage allowance? ▼
Each passenger is allowed one bag up to 20 kg and one small cabin bag up to 5 kg. Additional luggage is charged separately by the bus operator at the boarding point. Flyggo is not responsible for any luggage left unattended.
How early should I reach the boarding point? ▼
We recommend arriving at the boarding point at least 15 minutes before the scheduled departure. Buses depart on time, and seats may be forfeited if you are late. Use the Flyggo app for live bus tracking.
Is Flyggo available outside Tamil Nadu? ▼
Currently, Flyggo operates exclusively on Tamil Nadu intercity routes. We are expanding to Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh and Kerala soon. Stay tuned for updates on our website and social media channels.
